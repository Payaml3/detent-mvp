# Brand: Dekernai

Brand outputs (brand-skills, founder) land here. Name status: **Approved (B-01, 26 Nov 2026)**. Everything
below the name is **Proposed** until the founder approves it.

## Name

- **Dekernai**, said "deh-KER-nye". Write it as one word with one capital: Dekernai (not DekernAI, not DEKERNAI).
- Root: Latin *dēcernere*, "to decide, settle, rule on", from *dē-* ("off") and *cernere* ("to sift, see
  clearly"). The same root gives *decree*, *discern* and *certain*.
- Domains: dekernai.com (primary) and dekernai.co.uk, registered by the founder on 2026-11-26 (GoDaddy, 1 year). dekernai.ai and .io are not registered. Set up 2FA and the transfer lock; company email is still to choose.
- Evidence and alternatives considered: `docs/brand/name-review-2026-11.md`.

## Brand story (Proposed)

The Romans had a word for the moment a matter was weighed and settled: *dēcernere*, to sift and then
decide. Every deposit and payout a platform handles needs that moment, and on irreversible rails there is no
second chance. Dekernai is where it happens: AI agents do the groundwork, preparing, investigating and
reconciling, and the platform's own rules make the call. Sift every payment, settle it once, prove it after.

Maps to the three layers: **sift** (decision), **settle** (orchestration), **prove** (operations).

## Voice (Proposed)

- Precise, calm, plain. Short sentences. Say what happens to the money.
- Confident about mechanics, modest about outcomes: prototype items are labelled as prototypes.
- Technical where the reader is (heads of payments, compliance, engineers); never hype.

## Wording rules

- No invented clients, certifications or figures. Prototypes are labelled as prototypes.
- **The rules decide, never the AI.** Write "your rules decide", "agents prepare", "agents propose". Never
  "Dekernai's AI decides", "our AI approves" or "AI-powered approvals".
- Use "agentic payment controls" or "payment controls". Never "payments company" or "payment provider".
- A timeout is "unknown", never "failed"; a block is final.

## Visual identity

- Palette (Approved, B-02): Graphite and safety orange. Dark first, with a light mode for docs and the console.

  | Token | Hex | Use |
  |---|---|---|
  | Graphite | #18191B | Dark ground; ink on light grounds |
  | Steel | #232427 | Surfaces on dark |
  | Paper | #F3F3F1 | Light ground; ink on dark |
  | Safety orange | #FF4A1F | The one action colour: primary buttons, the mark, focus (black text on it) |
  | Orange text | #C2360F | Orange links and text on light grounds |
  | Unknown amber | #F2C94C | "Unknown" status |
  | Confirmed green | #237A46 | "Confirmed" status |

  Orange is the colour of the override switch: it only ever means "act here". Blocked is a graphite pill with a stop
  icon and its label, never orange and never colour alone. Every pairing passes WCAG AA (text 15.4, orange on graphite
  5.2, black on orange 5.9, orange text on paper 4.9).
- Fonts (D-18): Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono.
- Proposed: the detent-wheel motif (D-20) no longer fits the name; replacement idea "sift then settle"
  (many inputs filtering to one clear outcome). Decided in the design-system work.
- Logo (B-03): the **settled D**, a D monogram with one safety-orange point resting in its counter. In the
  logo the mark is the D of DekernAI (rest of the name in Space Grotesk Bold). Files and rules: `docs/brand/logo.md`; assets in `apps/site/public/brand/`.
