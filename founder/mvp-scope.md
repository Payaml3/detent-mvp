# MVP scope and proof

Generated by `tools/build_pages.py` from `spec/00-product-context.md`, `backlog/stories.yaml`,

**3 of 8 launch criteria fully built and tested.** In under 10 minutes, a head of payments can:

1. watch an AI agent turn an $18,500 supplier invoice into a USDC payment intent
2. see it escalated under their rules and approve it from the exceptions queue
3. watch a forced timeout resolve without a second payment
4. read the results report

Then a developer can get sandbox keys (sales-led), follow the quickstart and trigger every path with test values.

## Can be rough

- desktop only
- one demo login for the console
- a cold start
- several seconds of AI delay
- one currency per rail

## Out of scope for this MVP

- Real rails, banks or open banking
- integration with a client's custody provider
- real screening vendors
- card and voucher rails
- machine-learning risk scores
- treasury rebalancing
- certifications
- multi-tenant client production use
- single sign-on
- the investment-diligence workflow
