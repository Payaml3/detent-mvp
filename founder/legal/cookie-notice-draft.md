## The website (dekernai.com)

The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties: no analytics, advertising or social
trackers. Fonts and images come from our own site.

## The dashboard (app.dekernai.com)

The dashboard uses one first-party cookie:

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| [session cookie name] | Keeps you signed in and protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It is signed, HttpOnly, HTTPS-only and SameSite=Lax | [Until you sign out or it expires: confirm] |

It is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so it doesn't need your consent (PECR reg 6(4)). The
same cookie is set on the thank-you page after you send the access form, to protect the form; it holds no
tracking data.

## If this changes

If we add analytics, we'll name the provider and what it measures here, use it only in the ways the law
allows without consent, and give you an easy way to opt out; or we'll ask for consent first.

and an opt-out. Confirm before relying on it._
