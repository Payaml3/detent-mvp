DekernAI sits **beside** your payment rails, starting with the ones that can't be reversed: stablecoins and
instant bank payouts. Every payment goes through three layers:

| Layer | Question | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision** | Should this payment happen? | Your rules return EXECUTE, ESCALATE or BLOCK, with every check and its evidence |
| **Orchestration** | How and where does it go? | Picks a rail by your routing weights, sends exactly once, fails over only on technical errors |
| **Operations** | What happens around it? | An exceptions queue with owners and deadlines, reconciliation across rails, an audit log you can replay |

AI agents work across all three layers: they prepare payment intents, investigate escalations and draft
reconciliation fixes. **The AI proposes; your rules decide.** A model never approves, sends or retries a payment.

<Note>
DekernAI is agentic payment controls, not a payment provider. It never holds your funds. This is the public
**sandbox**: test networks and a simulated bank rail only. `sk_live_` keys are always refused.
</Note>

## Access

Access is not self-serve. Once DekernAI has approved your company, your account owner receives an invitation to
the dashboard, invites the team and creates sandbox keys. [Request sandbox access](https://dekernai.com/access/).
