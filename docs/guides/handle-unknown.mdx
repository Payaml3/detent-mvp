---
title: "Handle UNKNOWN payments"
description: "When a rail does not answer, the payment is UNKNOWN: never resend it, wait for the evidence."
---

A payout is `UNKNOWN` when DekernAI sent it and the rail did not answer in time. The money may have moved. It is
not a failure, and treating it as one is how payouts get paid twice.

## Try it

In auto mode, send a payout to `cpt_lumen` for an amount ending in `.13`, such as `90.13`:

```json unknown.json
{
  "kind": "payout",
  "source": "platform",
  "counterparty_id": "cpt_lumen",
  "destination": {"kind": "evm_wallet", "network": "base-sepolia", "destination_id": "dst_lumen_base"},
  "amount": {"amount": "90.13", "currency": "USDC"}
}
```

The simulated chain takes the payout and does not answer, so the payment becomes `UNKNOWN` and
`payment_intent.unknown` is sent. A little later DekernAI finds the transfer on the chain, and the payment
becomes `CONFIRMED`, with a single attempt.

## What your platform should do

- **Don't resend.** Don't create a new payment intent for the same payout, and don't retry with a new
  `Idempotency-Key`. DekernAI never sends again while any attempt is UNKNOWN.
- **Keep the user's funds reserved.** An UNKNOWN payout keeps its ledger reservation.
- **Show it as pending** to your user, not as failed.
- **Wait for the evidence.** DekernAI checks the rail until it has a definitive answer, and then sends
  `payment_intent.confirmed` or `payment_intent.failed`. Only definitive rail evidence settles it.
- **If it lasts,** an exception case opens for your team with what is known.

Use `sequence` on webhook events to tell the latest state, or poll `GET /v1/payment_intents/{id}`. See
[Payment states](/concepts/payment-states#unknown-is-not-failed).
