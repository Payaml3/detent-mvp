---
title: "Let an AI agent pay a supplier invoice"
description: "An agent proposes an $18,500 USDC payout under its mandate; your rules decide; a person approves."
---

Your accounts-payable agent reads a supplier invoice from Bluefin Components Ltd (fictional) for 18,500.00 USDC.
The agent proposes the payout. It can't send it, approve it or change your rules: the mandate says what it may
ask for, and your policy decides.

Your sandbox organisation comes with the agent's mandate, `mnd_demo_ap_agent`: it may pay `cpt_bluefin` and
`cpt_harbor`, up to 20,000.00 USDC a payment and 50,000.00 USDC in total, on the test networks. Read it with
`GET /v1/mandates`. The guide assumes your organisation is in auto mode; see
[Move from shadow to auto mode](/guides/shadow-to-auto).

## 1. Give the agent its own key

Create a key in the dashboard (**Keys**) with only the `intents:create` scope, and give it to the agent. Its
payouts are recorded as initiated by the member who owns the key, so that member can't approve them later.

## 2. The agent proposes the payout

The agent sends a payment intent with `"source": "agent"` and its mandate, and the invoice number as the
reference:

```json agent-intent.json
{
  "kind": "payout",
  "source": "agent",
  "mandate_id": "mnd_demo_ap_agent",
  "counterparty_id": "cpt_bluefin",
  "destination": {"kind": "evm_wallet", "network": "base-sepolia", "destination_id": "dst_bluefin_base"},
  "amount": {"amount": "18500.00", "currency": "USDC"},
  "reference": "INV-2026-0142"
}
```

## 3. Read the decision

The mandate check passes, because Bluefin is in scope and 18,500.00 is under the per-payment limit. Two of
your rules escalate it: it is the first payment to Bluefin, and it is above your auto limit of 10,000.00 USDC.

```json escalated
{
  "status": "ESCALATED",
  "decision": {
    "outcome": "ESCALATE",
    "checks": [
      {"check": "mandate_scope", "result": "PASS"},
      {"check": "auto_limit", "result": "ESCALATE", "reason": "ABOVE_AUTO_LIMIT"},
      {"check": "first_payment", "result": "ESCALATE", "reason": "FIRST_PAYMENT"}
    ]
  }
}
```

An exception case opens, with an owner and a deadline, and `exception.created` is sent to your webhooks.

## 4. A person approves

A reviewer or your payments lead checks the invoice against the case and approves it, in the console or with
[the API](/guides/approve-escalations). Approval runs your rules again; when nothing new has come up, the
payment becomes `READY` and is sent, and `payment_intent.confirmed` follows. The mandate's `used` total goes up
by 18,500.00.

## What the agent can't do

Ask for more than the mandate allows and the payout is blocked at once, with the reason:

```json over-mandate.json
{
  "kind": "payout",
  "source": "agent",
  "mandate_id": "mnd_demo_ap_agent",
  "counterparty_id": "cpt_bluefin",
  "destination": {"kind": "evm_wallet", "network": "base-sepolia", "destination_id": "dst_bluefin_base"},
  "amount": {"amount": "20000.01", "currency": "USDC"},
  "reference": "INV-2026-0143"
}
```

returns `BLOCKED` with `MANDATE_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED`. A block is final: it is never approved, retried or resent. The
same goes for a payee outside the mandate (`MANDATE_PAYEE_OUT_OF_SCOPE`) or a revoked mandate
(`MANDATE_REVOKED`). See [Mandates](/concepts/mandates).
