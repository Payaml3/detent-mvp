---
title: "Use the kill switch"
description: "Stop every payout at once, and release it when you are ready."
---

The kill switch stops every payout in your organisation. It is checked inside the send lock just before each
send, so nothing leaves once it is engaged. Decisions still run and are recorded; a payment that is cleared to
send is held with `KILL_SWITCH_ENGAGED` instead.

Both calls need a key with the `admin` scope that belongs to your payments lead: only the payments lead engages
and releases it.

## Engage

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$DEKERNAI_BASE_URL/v1/kill_switch" -H "Authorization: Bearer $LEAD_KEY"
```

```json engaged
{"engaged": true}
```

`kill_switch.engaged` is sent to your webhooks. New payouts and payouts already waiting are held:

```json
{"status": "HELD"}
```

## Release

```bash
curl -s -X DELETE "$DEKERNAI_BASE_URL/v1/kill_switch" -H "Authorization: Bearer $LEAD_KEY"
```

```json released
{"engaged": false}
```

Held payments go through the pre-send checks again, including your rules and the agent's mandate, before they
are sent. `kill_switch.released` is sent to your webhooks.

<Note>Engaging and releasing are in the audit log with the member and their role. The console has the same switch,
on every screen.</Note>
