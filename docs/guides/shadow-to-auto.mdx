---
title: "Move from shadow to auto mode"
description: "Watch your rules decide in shadow mode, then let them send, with two people agreeing."
---

Every organisation starts in **shadow mode**: each payment is decided and recorded, and nothing is sent. It lets
you run DekernAI beside your current payouts and compare.

| Mode | What happens after the decision |
|---|---|
| `shadow` | Recorded only. The status is `SHADOWED` |
| `assist` | Cleared payments wait for a person to click Send, who can't be the person who created them |
| `auto` | Cleared payments are sent |

## 1. Review the shadow decisions

```bash
curl -s "$DEKERNAI_BASE_URL/v1/payment_intents?status=SHADOWED" -H "Authorization: Bearer $DEKERNAI_KEY"
```

Each one has the full decision: the outcome and every check with its evidence. Compare them with what your team
did. Where a rule escalates too often or not enough, change it in the console's **Policy** screen.

## 2. Change the mode

A mode change needs two people: your payments lead and your compliance lead. One proposes it and the other
approves it.

1. In the dashboard, open **Console**, then **Settings**.
2. One of them chooses `auto` (or `assist`), gives a reason and proposes.
3. The other opens **Settings** and approves.

Both steps are in the audit log. From then on the same request that returned `SHADOWED` returns `READY` and is
sent:

```json auto
{"status": "READY"}
```

The mode is read again inside the send lock, so a payment cleared under auto waits for a click if the mode has
moved to assist, and nothing is sent once you go back to shadow.
