---
title: "Exceptions and deadlines"
description: "Every escalation is a case with an owner, a backup and a deadline. A deadline never approves anything."
---

When a decision is ESCALATE, DekernAI opens an **exception case**. Its owner is set by your rules: by default a
reviewer owns payout cases, with your backup owner behind them, and your payments lead owns `UNKNOWN_STUCK` cases.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `type` | What needs a decision: a payout, a deposit, a stuck UNKNOWN payment, a reconciliation break |
| `owner` | The person who should decide |
| `deadline_at` | When it is due. By default 2 hours for a payout above the auto limit, 4 hours for other payouts and 1 business day for a deposit |
| `status` | `OPEN`, `HELD_OVERDUE` or `RESOLVED` |

The case shows the raw facts first (the intent, every check and its evidence), then what the investigation agent
found, kept separate and labelled as the agent's.

## Approving and rejecting

A reviewer approves or rejects in the console or with `POST /v1/exceptions/{id}/approve` and
`POST /v1/exceptions/{id}/reject`, with a `reason`. Two rules always apply:

- **The person who created the payment can't approve it.**
- **Approval re-runs the rules** with the active version. The re-run executes only if every remaining reason is one
  the approver saw and its evidence is unchanged. A new reason, or a changed destination, opens a new case. A BLOCK
  is never approved.

## When the deadline passes

Nothing is approved by waiting. An overdue case becomes `HELD_OVERDUE`: the payment stays held, the backup owner
takes over and the counterparty is told about the delay. The case stays until a person decides it.

## Getting told

The `exception.created`, `exception.overdue` and `exception.resolved` webhooks follow each case, and
`GET /v1/exceptions` lists them with filters for `status`, `owner`, `tier` and `overdue`.
