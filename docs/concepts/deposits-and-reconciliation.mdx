## Deposit intents

`POST /v1/deposit_intents` creates deposit instructions for one of your users' accounts:

```json
{"account_ref": "acct_u1001", "currency": "USDC", "network": "base-sepolia",
 "expected_amount": {"amount": "250.00", "currency": "USDC"}}
```

The response gives the payer what to send: a test wallet address on a chain, or a payment reference on the
simulated bank rail. Instructions expire after 7 days by default. Creating one is idempotent, like a payment intent.

## Crediting

DekernAI watches each rail for money coming in. An inbound transfer is credited to the user's balance only when:

- the token and network match the instructions (otherwise BLOCK);
- the sender passes screening (otherwise BLOCK);
- the amount is within your tolerance of the expected amount, exact by default (otherwise ESCALATE).

A credited deposit posts to the ledger and sends `deposit.credited`. One that fails a check goes to a suspense
account and opens a deposit case (`deposit.exception`), to be credited or refunded by a person.

<Note>Inbound transfers need a real test network: the simulated chain has no deposits.</Note>

## The ledger

Every money movement is a balanced, append-only double-entry ledger entry: a payout reserves funds when it is
submitted, settles when confirmed and is reversed if it finally fails. An UNKNOWN payout keeps its reservation.
Corrections are new entries; nothing is edited or deleted.

## Reconciliation

A reconciliation run compares the ledger with what each rail recorded (chain transfers and bank statements) and
reports the match rate and every **break**: a payout missing on the rail, an inbound transfer nobody claimed, a
rail record the ledger never booked. Each break sends `reconciliation.break`. The reconciliation agent may draft
a fix; your payments lead approves it in the console, and the fix is a new ledger entry.

Read the results with `GET /v1/reconciliation/runs` and `GET /v1/reconciliation/breaks`.
