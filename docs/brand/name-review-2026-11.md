# Brand name review: is "Detent" the best name?

Advisory, 25 November 2026. Nothing in the repo was renamed. Checks are rough screens, not legal clearance:
a UK trade mark attorney search is still needed before committing (the `legal` gate), and buying any domain
needs the `spend` gate.

## Recommendation

1. **First, confirm who owns "Detent Technology Group Ltd".** A UK trade mark application for **DETENT**
   (filed, classes 9, 16, 35, 42 and 45: software, SaaS and related services) stands in that company's name.
   It sits in a group of UK software companies incorporated in September 2026 (Detent Topco Ltd, Detent
   Technology Group Ltd, Detent GTM OS Ltd, all at one Milton Keynes address).
2. **If that is you:** keep Detent. It is the best fit of anything screened. Register `detent.io` and
   `detent.co` now (both appear unregistered) and treat `detent.com` (for sale on Afternic) as a later purchase.
3. **If it is not you:** drop Detent and move to **Pawl**. It keeps the whole brand idea and the site's wheel
   (D-20), and it was the cleanest name on the trade mark screen. Runner-up: **Countersign**.

## Why Detent works (on the merits)

- A detent is the catch that holds a mechanism in place until enough force releases it. That is the product:
  a payment is held until the client's rules release it, one tooth at a time, and a block is final.
- Short, two syllables, says "control" and "precision" rather than "payments company", which suits the
  positioning rule that Detent is never a payment provider.
- The site design is already built around it (D-20: the halftone detent wheel whose catch releases one tooth
  when a payment is sent).
- Weaknesses: an uncommon word; confusable with *détente*; stress is ambiguous (dee-TENT vs DEE-tent).

## Ruled out early

- **Interlock:** strong meaning, but it is the name of an active ransomware group (FBI and CISA advisory
  AA25-203A). Unusable for a security-sensitive payments product.
- **Arbiter:** Castellum.AI sells "Arbiter", AI agents that resolve AML and payment compliance alerts. Same buyer.
- **Tenet, Ratify, Writ, Verdict:** the .ai domains are all long taken and the words are crowded.
- **Escapement:** perfect watch-movement metaphor, but too long and hard to say.
- **Anything with pay, bank, vault, escrow or custody:** implies Detent holds funds or is a payment provider,
  which the positioning forbids.

## Note on a naming neighbour

Cordant (New York and Miami, $8m seed, July 2026) describes itself as a command centre for payments oversight
across fragmented infrastructure. Not a name conflict, but a close positioning neighbour worth adding to the
competitor work.

# Round 2: names that carry "agentic" or "AI" (25 Nov 2026, after the founder's feedback)

The founder didn't like Detent, Pawl or Countersign and asked for a name that brings in agentic or AI and
helps search.

## How much a name helps search

- Search engines rank pages on content, titles and links, not on the brand word. The terms buyers type are
  phrases like "agentic payments", "payment controls" or "payout approval", not a brand. So the biggest search
  win is owning the **category phrase**, "agentic payment controls", in the page title, H1, docs and posts.
- **agenticpaymentcontrols.com is unregistered.** Registering it (spend gate) and pointing it at the site, or
  using it for a guide page, captures the exact phrase whatever the brand is.
- "Agent" names are saturated: of about 70 agent or AI .com names checked, almost all were registered,
  most in 2024 to 2026 (agentguard, agentgate, agentclear, agentlock, agentcontrols, agenticcontrols, agentrule,
  mandateos, agenticpayments, agenticpay and more). A pure "Agentic X" name would also date fast.

## Shortlist

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

# Deep check: Kybai (25 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: usable, with one real caveat.** Domains are almost all free and no live company uses the name.
But to payments and compliance buyers, "Kybai" reads as **"KYB AI"** (Know Your Business, a standard
compliance check, plus AI). Decide whether that helps or hurts before committing.

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |

| Free domains | **kybai.ai, .io, .co, .eu, .co.uk, .net, .org, .app, .dev**; getkybai.com, usekybai.com, trykybai.com, kybaihq.com, kyb-ai.com |

| US trade marks (aggregators) | No KYBAI or "KYB AI" found. Nearby: **KYAI** (ALT5 Sigma, a crypto and fintech company), filed May 2026, classes 9, 36, 42, pending. **KYB** (KYB Corporation, Japanese shock-absorber maker), US and WIPO marks in classes 9 and 12. KYG.AI, pending, 9, 36, 42 |
| UK and EU trade marks | **Not checked.** TMview still blocks automated searches; UKIPO and WIPO need a human CAPTCHA |
| UK companies | No "Kybai". Close: **KYBA AI LTD** (active since 2020, software SIC codes, no website found); KYBERNAI LTD (Oct 2026, software); KYBERNA AI CONSULTING LTD (Jun 2026). The *kyberna-* + AI idea is in use |
| Neighbours | Kyriba (treasury and payments software) markets an agentic AI; different name but the same "Ky-" start in the same market |
| The KYB reading | KYB is a core compliance term in this market, and Detent's policy runs KYB checks. Upside: compliance buyers recognise it. Downside: people may assume Detent is a KYB verification vendor (Sumsub, Persona, Trulioo), and search engines may treat "kybai" as "KYB AI" and show those vendors' pages |
| Meaning | Greek *kybernan*, "to steer, govern" (root of cyber and govern). No negative meaning found in a quick check; not reviewed by native speakers of other EU languages |

## What to do if you choose it

1. Hand-search "kybai", "kyb ai" and "kyba" on TMview (tmdn.org/tmview), classes 9, 36 and 42, or ask a
   trade mark attorney (legal gate).
2. Register kybai.ai and kybai.co.uk at least (spend gate); ask Afternic for the kybai.com price.
3. Write it **Kybai** (not KybAI) so it isn't read as an acronym, and tell the "steer" story on the About page.
