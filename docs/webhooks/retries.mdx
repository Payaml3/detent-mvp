---
title: "Retries and ordering"
description: "At-least-once delivery, the retry schedule and what to do with duplicates."
---

A delivery is an HTTPS `POST` of the event, with a 10-second timeout and no redirects. Any `2xx` answer is
success. Anything else (another status, a timeout, a connection error) is retried after **1 minute, 5 minutes,
30 minutes, 2 hours, 6 hours and 24 hours**, and then marked failed.

## At least once

The same event can arrive more than once, for example when DekernAI sent it but did not record your answer.
Deduplicate on the event `id`.

## Ordering

Events can arrive out of order, especially across retries. Each event's `sequence` increases per payment: keep the
highest you have processed and ignore anything lower, or read the payment with `GET /v1/payment_intents/{id}` for
its current state.

## Seeing and resending deliveries

`GET /v1/webhook_endpoints/{id}/deliveries` lists every attempt with its status code, response time and outcome;
the dashboard's **Webhooks** page shows the same. `POST /v1/webhook_endpoints/{id}/resend` with an `event_id` sends
one event again.
